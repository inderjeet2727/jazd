British asian singer and performer

Jaz Dhami known to uphold and blend his Punjabi roots with modern R&B, hip-hop and EDM.

Beyond the music, Jaz’s journey reflects resilience and purpose. After privately battling cancer and facing personal loss, he continues to inspire with his authenticity, powerful voice, and boundary-breaking style.
